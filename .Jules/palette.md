## 2024-05-15 - Icon-only buttons lacking accessibility attributes
**Learning:** Found several icon-only buttons (`<i>` tags without text inside `<button>`) that were missing both `aria-label` and `title` attributes. This makes them invisible to screen readers and difficult to understand for users who rely on hover states. The pattern is widespread across the app in various components like modals, feeds, and settings. Also, the `<i>` tags should have `aria-hidden="true"` to prevent screen readers from reading the icon class.
**Action:** Created a script to find such buttons and manually added `aria-label` (for screen readers), `title` (for tooltips on hover), and `aria-hidden="true"` on the inner `<i>` tag across multiple files (`search.html`, `admin.html`, `emp.html`, `health-manager/script.js`).
